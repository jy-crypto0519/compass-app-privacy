概述

Overview

指南针（以下简称"本应用"）是一款极简精准的罗盘工具。我们高度重视您的个人隐私，本隐私政策说明了我们如何处理您的信息。

Compass (hereinafter referred to as "the App") is a simple and accurate compass tool. We highly value your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

我们收集的信息

Information We Collect

✅ 核心原则：本应用不上传任何个人数据到服务器。

✅ Core Principle: The App does not upload any personal data to servers.

  • 位置信息（GPS）：当您授权位置访问后，本应用会获取您的经纬度和海拔，仅用于： Location Information (GPS): When you grant location access, the App receives your latitude, longitude, and altitude, used solely for:
    • 显示当前坐标和海拔
    • Displaying current coordinates and altitude
    • 提供真北（True Heading）方向计算
    • Providing True Heading direction calculation
    • 让您保存的点位标记能够指引方向
    • Enabling saved location marks for direction guidance
  • 磁力计 / 陀螺仪数据：仅用于检测方向和手机倾斜状态，实现罗盘和水平仪功能。 Magnetometer / Gyroscope Data: Used solely for detecting direction and device tilt, enabling compass and level features.
  • 点位标记数据：您保存的点位存储在设备本地（应用沙盒的 JSON 文件中），不会上传。 Location Mark Data: Your saved location marks are stored locally on the device (in the app sandbox as a JSON file) and never uploaded.

数据存储

Data Storage

  • 所有数据仅存储在您的设备本地（应用沙盒内）
  • All data is stored locally on your device (within the app sandbox)
  • 卸载应用即可完全清除所有数据
  • Uninstalling the app completely removes all data
  • 不使用任何云端存储
  • No cloud storage is used

第三方服务

Third-Party Services

本应用不使用任何第三方分析、广告、追踪或崩溃报告服务。

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting services.

您的权利

Your Rights

  • 您可以随时在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 定位服务」中关闭位置权限
  • You can disable location permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services
  • 您可以随时删除本应用以清除所有本地数据
  • You can delete the App at any time to erase all local data

儿童隐私

Children's Privacy

本应用不收集任何个人身份信息，适合所有年龄段用户使用。

The App does not collect any personally identifiable information and is suitable for users of all ages.
